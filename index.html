About

I'm an integrated M.S./Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Seung Ah Lee in the Imaging Intelligence Laboratory.

My research focuses on computational imaging, computer vision, and physics-guided deep learning, with particular interests in lensless reconstruction and biomedical imaging. I develop learning-based methods that connect image-formation models with neural networks to recover visual information from unconventional imaging systems. I received my B.S. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Yonsei University in 2024.
